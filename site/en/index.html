Get started

Download, drag, start copying

No sign-up and nothing to set up. Kippet starts keeping what you copy as soon as it opens.

Install

Open the DMG and drag Kippet into your Applications folder.

Open

A small tag appears in the menu bar and Kippet starts recording. Allow clipboard access when macOS asks.

Open the drawer, paste

Press ⌥⇧V in any text field, pick a card and press Return.

To paste straight into the original field when you press Return, turn on Kippet in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. It works without that too: the item is already on your clipboard, so just press ⌘V.
Features

It keeps things, and pastes them right

Every common action has a shortcut, so your hands stay on the keyboard.

A drawer at the bottom

Scan horizontal cards at a glance. Arrow keys select, Space previews, typing searches, and ⌘1–9 pastes the first nine.

Paste with a transform

Press ⌥↩ to trim whitespace, format JSON or strip tracking parameters, or paste as plain text, Markdown or HTML.

Knows what it holds

Colors show a swatch, code is highlighted and one-time codes stand out. Text and QR codes in images are recognized on your Mac and searchable.

Pinboards and snippets

Star what you reuse and file it on colored pinboards. Save a snippet with ⌘N and paste it whenever you need it.

Rules

File, star or expire clips by source or content. One-time codes are cleared after 2 minutes, and password manager copies are skipped.

Paste stack

Collect a batch first, then press ⌃⌥V in the target field to paste them one after another.

Sync across Macs

Choose an iCloud Drive or other cloud folder to sync stars, pinboards and snippets. Regular history never leaves the Mac.

Shortcuts and AI

kippet:// links work with Shortcuts and Raycast. Switch it on and Claude or Cursor can search your history through a local MCP server.

Privacy

What you copy stays on your Mac

Kippet has no account, no server, and collects no usage data.

  • Stored on this MacHistory lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Kippet and is never uploaded.
  • Sensitive copies skippedPassword manager copies are skipped by default. Pause recording or exclude an app at any time.
  • Sync uses your own folderOnly stars, pinboards and snippets sync. History and one-time codes stay on this Mac.
  • Online only when you askKippet visits a web page only when you click Load link preview.

FAQ

You might be wondering

What do I need to run it?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon or Intel.

What language is the app in?

The app's interface is currently in Simplified Chinese. Shortcuts, content and pasting work the same in any language.

Why does it ask for Accessibility access?

Only to press ⌘V for you after you pick a card, so the item lands in the original field. Kippet works without it; just press ⌘V yourself.

Will it save my passwords?

Password managers mark their copies as confidential, and Kippet skips them by default. Before copying anything else sensitive, pause recording from the menu bar or exclude the app in Settings.

How much does it keep?

1,000 history items by default, adjustable by count or by days in Settings. Starred and pinned items don't count toward the limit.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Kippet from the menu bar and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove your history too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Kippet.

Never lose something you copied again

Drag it into Applications, open it, and you're set.
